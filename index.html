About Moriah Medical Clinic

Moriah Medical Clinic is a family practice medical clinic located in Red Deer County available for booked appointments only.

Our team is a group of experienced family physicians providing care across all scopes of general practice, including general exams, complete physicals, driver's medicals, and family health. We are dedicated to providing excellent and high-quality care to every patient, and we are proud to serve our local community in Central Alberta.

We are unable to accommodate walk-in visits as our schedule is fully booked each day. If you do not have a family doctor, please call the clinic and we can book a meet-and-greet appointment to help you become a patient with one of our physicians who are accepting new patients.

We are closed on Sundays and statutory holidays.

Dr. Memuna Amba-Ambaiowei is a highly qualified and compassionate Family Physician dedicated to providing comprehensive, patient-centered care across all stages of life. Grounded in a strong scientific foundation with a Bachelor of Science (BSc) in Human Anatomy, she earned her Medical Degree (MBBS) and went on to achieve postgraduate designations in both the United Kingdom (MRCGP) and Canada (CCFP).

As a fully licensed physician with the College of Physicians and Surgeons of Alberta (CPSA), Dr. Amba-Ambaiowei brings a wealth of international experience to her practice. She has developed specialized clinical interests designed to support families through their most critical milestones. Her areas of focus include women’s health—with a dedicated emphasis on perinatal and postpartum care—as well as child health, mental health support, elderly care, and compassionate palliative medicine. Dr. Amba-Ambaiowei is passionate about building long-term relationships with her patients, combining clinical excellence with deep empathy to guide them through every chapter of their healthcare journey.

Dr. Tari Amba-Ambaiowei is a dedicated, compassionate and internationally trained Family Medicine Physician with advanced training in public health and tropical medicine, bringing a broad global health perspective to clinical practice. Licensed with the College of Physicians and Surgeons of Alberta (CPSA), He is experienced in delivering patient-centred, evidence-based care across diverse healthcare settings, with a strong commitment to preventative medicine, community health, and multidisciplinary collaboration.

His clinical interests include Acute and Urgent Care Medicine, Maternal and Perinatal Health, Preventive and Population Health, Evidence-based Primary Care, Chronic Disease Management, Mental Health, Health Equity and Underserved Populations. He is passionate about improving healthcare access, supporting vulnerable communities and integrating public health principles into frontline clinical practice. With a strong academic foundation - spanning an MBBS, MSc in Public Health (UoL), Diploma in Tropical Medicine (LSHTM) and dual Family Physician Certifications (MRCGP, CCFP)— Dr. Amba-Ambaiowei integrates multidisciplinary expertise in delivering high‐quality, culturally informed medical care.
