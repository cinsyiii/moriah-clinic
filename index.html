About Moriah Medical Clinic

Moriah Medical Clinic is a newly established family practice medical clinic in Red Deer County accepting new patients, available for both walk-ins and booked appointments.

Our team is a group of experienced family physicians providing care across all scopes of general practice, including general exams, complete physicals, driver's medicals, family health, and minor procedures. We are dedicated to providing excellent and high-quality care to every patient, and we are proud to serve our local community in Central Alberta.
